Stage 2: Paint the Setting & Introduce Your Team

Goal for this part: Create a vivid picture of the location and introduce your hero and companion working together. Use a Simile or Personification to make the environment come alive!

Chunk A: Paint the Atmosphere (3 Senses)
TRY A SIMILE: "like a..." or "as... as a..." TRY PERSONIFICATION: Give weather human actions
Describe what your characters see, hear, and feel. Try describing the location or weather using a simile or personification.
Chunk B: Show Your Team Interacting (with Dialogue)
TOOL: Dialogue ("...") + Character Actions
Show your hero and companion working together. Describe their movements and give them spoken dialogue.
Chunk C: Show the Reaction of Other Characters
TOOL: Show, Don't Tell
Show how nearby background characters (villagers, guards, animals) react to the scene.

Stage 3: Build the Danger & Trigger the Trap

Goal for this part: Build suspense before the big problem hits! Use Onomatopoeia (sound words) and show how your hero's body reacts to fear.

Chunk A: The Warning Sign (Build Suspense)
TRY ONOMATOPOEIA: CRACK!, RUMBLE!, SNAP!
Describe something changing before the danger appears. Is it a strange noise, lights flickering, or a sudden cold wind?
Chunk B: The Trap or Threat Hits
TOOL: Compound Sentence (, but / , so)
How does the villain strike, or how does the trap slam shut? Show how your team is caught off guard.
Chunk C: Body Language (Show Fear)
TRY A METAPHOR: "His heart was a pounding drum..."
Show how scared your hero is without using the word 'scared'. What happens to their heart, breath, or hands?

Stage 4: Write the Final Showdown & Action Climax

Goal for this part: Write an intense fight or standoff between your hero, companion, and rival. Use Hyperbole (exaggeration) and vivid action verbs to power the scene!

Chunk A: The Verbal Standoff & Dialogue
TOOL: Powerful Dialogue & Tagged Speech
Write spoken dialogue exchanged between the hero and antagonist before the action strikes.
Chunk B: Describe the High-Energy Action
TRY HYPERBOLE: Exaggerate the power or scale
Describe micro-actions in dramatic detail. Use strong action verbs and hyperbole to emphasize power.
Chunk C: Show the Teamwork Turning Point
TRY A SIMILE: Describe the turning moment using a comparison
How do the hero and companion combine their skills to turn the tide and defeat the challenge?

Stage 5: Show What Happens Right After the Victory

Goal for this part: Describe the quiet moment after the battle, show how the scenery turns from scary to safe, and bring your characters together to celebrate!

Chunk A: The Scene Changes from Scary to Safe
TRY A METAPHOR: "The sky became a..."
Describe how the weather, sky, or room changes right after the villain is defeated. Use warm, peaceful words.
Chunk B: The Characters Celebrate Together
TOOL: Group Dialogue & Relief Actions
Show the hero, companion, and background characters sharing a sigh of relief, high-fiving, or speaking to each other.
Chunk C: Write a Reflective Closing Sentence
TOOL: Complex Sentence (Although...)
How has your hero grown or changed? End with a strong final thought about their future.
